Let’s see if money is waiting for you

About two minutes. No cost, no pressure.

How should we reach you?

Free, no obligation. We will never ask for your Social Security number to check.

What happens next

1

We look

Tell us where and roughly when the home was sold. We search public court, trustee and county records to see if surplus funds exist in your name. Free.

2

We explain

If there’s money, we tell you how much the records show, who is holding it, and every option you have, including filing on your own.

3

Attorneys file

If you want our help, you sign a clear written agreement first. Where the law requires it, an independent licensed attorney prepares and files the claim.

4

You get paid

The court, trustee or county releases the funds. Our fee, if any, is only taken from a successful recovery, as stated in your agreement.
